The sample reference transition at frames 99?114 crosses the end of C0001056 local ID 1 and the beginning of ID 9. The original tracked boxes coexist in frames 107, 109 and 110. A research-only handoff proposal requires the earlier track to start and end earlier, 3?10 shared frames, at least three shared frames with IoU >= 0.6, and no competing predecessor or successor. Thresholds are exploratory and there is no appearance or identity guarantee. On the fixed sample this proposes C0001056 ID 1 -> ID 9, with median IoU 0.684. At IoU 0.5 an additional C0001055 ID 4 -> ID 10 proposal appears, illustrating threshold sensitivity.

The experiment joins only research hypotheses containing the proposed local IDs; the existing cross-camera association groups are otherwise unchanged. Every original source observation is retained exactly once. Same-camera simultaneous sources are treated as an unresolved conflict rather than counted twice; cross-view points beyond 0.5 scene units also remain conflicts. No gaps are interpolated. The fixed sample yields eight experimental groups instead of nine, which is not a people-count improvement or proof that the proposed link is correct.

The handoff alone therefore does not solve the event-coverage problem; missing pose, quality exclusions and position conflicts must be investigated. The experimental grouping is not used by the default page or strict quality gate.
