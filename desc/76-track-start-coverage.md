# Track-start coverage investigation

2026-11-09. On the previously examined site03 007 example, a reference route crosses the illustrative north/south boundary near frames 358?364. Existing two-camera direct hypotheses do not produce a transition. Inspecting all six cameras' existing pose-projection caches found no reference-near projected observations in the four previously unused cameras during frames 330?375. In C0001056, projected detections near that reference exist at frames 344?345 before local ID 47 first appears in the filtered floor-position artifact at frame 346. Subsequent full tracker replay shows that ID 47 actually begins at frame 338; see correction below. This finding uses the already-inspected reference and is not independent validation.

An exploratory question is whether a conservative, same-camera track-start evidence lead can recover early detections that a tracker has not yet assigned. Any candidate must use raw detection and tracker evidence only, preserve existing observations, remain separate from confirmed identities and business counts, and expose ambiguity and source frames. Consecutive prior raw boxes and a tracked anchor may be compared by a fixed overlap rule, but uniqueness is only among surviving projected detections; suppressed or unprojectable detections remain unknown. The 007 example motivated this investigation, so selecting thresholds or reporting improvement on it cannot establish generalization. Evaluate any prototype on other recordings, multiple tracks, false-link risks and independent labels before integration or changing event rules. Missing north dwell and cross-camera handoff are separate issues; adding start evidence alone cannot prove a valid transition.


## Preliminary same-camera predecessor probe (interpretation corrected below)

An ignored local prototype, data/processed/site03-007-start-probe.py, explored the already-inspected two-camera 007 data. It matched each tracked floor observation to a surviving raw projected detection at the same frame by numerical position, then looked backward at most two consecutive frames, requiring adjacent box IoU at least 0.7 and uniqueness among surviving projected detections. It discarded candidate detections already represented by tracked floor observations and candidates claimed by multiple track starts. This is an exploratory rule chosen after inspecting this example, not a frozen held-out evaluation; unprojectable or filtered detections were not included in its ambiguity check.

The probe found 14 predecessor leads: six from C0001055 and eight from C0001056. Among those, 12 and their track-start anchors were each uniquely within 0.5 scene units of the same published reference ID; two were unresolved. This reference-proximity check is neither independently verified camera identity nor false-link accuracy. The two leads before C0001056 track 47 occur at frames 344 and 345, with adjacent box IoU 0.872 and 0.833. This can supply earlier review evidence but does not resolve the later cross-camera handoff or establish a region transition. No observations, IDs, event counts or product behavior were altered.

The ignored output data/processed/site03-007-start-probe.json records candidate coordinates, IoUs and script hash, but the prototype does not yet preserve a complete machine-readable input-hash ledger or independent review and must not be treated as a production-ready importer. Before reuse: harden provenance and ambiguity handling for all detections, add meaningful tests, evaluate other clips without selecting thresholds on their references, and expose tentative evidence without silently joining identities.


## Correction after raw tracker replay

Replaying the original pinned video, YOLO11s-pose weights and ByteTrack configuration through frame 360 shows C0001056 local ID 47 first appears at frame 338, with tracker outputs at frames 340?342 and 346?349. Thus frame 346 was the first *eligible projected observation* in the filtered floor artifact, not the start of the actual tracker. Frames 344?345 should be investigated as observations near a tracker/geometry coverage gap, not as two pre-start detections. The earlier proposed start-recovery explanation was incorrect.

A separate replay of the complete raw model detections at the 14 proposed frame pairs found all 14 had exactly one overlapping candidate in each direction at IoU 0.7 among that model's detections at confidence 0.1. This includes detections excluded from the floor-projection cache, but still excludes people the detector missed or suppressed; overlap and reference proximity do not prove identity. The raw tracker and ambiguity reports are ignored local files data/processed/site03-007-tracker-start-check.json and data/processed/site03-007-start-ambiguity.json, with corresponding ignored scripts. No candidate has been merged into the tracked output.

Next investigation should model missing tracked observations and geometry eligibility explicitly, distinguish tracker gaps from projection gaps and actual starts, preserve all source observations, and test ambiguity and false links on other recordings. Do not integrate the earlier start-recovery prototype as written.


## Full tracker-versus-floor replay

A subsequent replay ran the same pinned model and tracker over all 500 frames of each original 007 camera and checked that every existing eligible floor observation's (frame, local ID) was present in the replay. C0001055 yielded 332 tracked box observations and 203 eligible floor observations: 5 local track IDs, 4 with any eligible floor point, and none of those 4 had a delayed first eligible point. C0001056 yielded 513 tracked box observations and 334 eligible floor observations: 11 local track IDs, 9 with any eligible floor point, and 5 of those 9 had a delayed first eligible point (median delay across the 9 was 2 frames). These are pipeline coverage counts, not verified people, recall or proof that the excluded boxes were correct. The eligible floor set also includes duplicate and geometry/ankle exclusions; the figures alone do not isolate individual exclusion causes.

The ignored local data/processed/site03-007-tracker-coverage.json includes per-ID start delays and verified video, weights, floor-artifact and script hashes; the companion script is an exploratory replay, not a versioned pipeline. The result strengthens the requirement that any review or recovery workflow distinguish tracker observations, floor eligibility and actual identity instead of using the first floor point as a track start.
