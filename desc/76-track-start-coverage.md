An exploratory question is whether a conservative, same-camera track-start evidence lead can recover early detections that a tracker has not yet assigned. Any candidate must use raw detection and tracker evidence only, preserve existing observations, remain separate from confirmed identities and business counts, and expose ambiguity and source frames. Consecutive prior raw boxes and a tracked anchor may be compared by a fixed overlap rule, but uniqueness is only among surviving projected detections; suppressed or unprojectable detections remain unknown. The 007 example motivated this investigation, so selecting thresholds or reporting improvement on it cannot establish generalization. Evaluate any prototype on other recordings, multiple tracks, false-link risks and independent labels before integration or changing event rules. Missing north dwell and cross-camera handoff are separate issues; adding start evidence alone cannot prove a valid transition.


## Preliminary same-camera predecessor probe

An ignored local prototype, data/processed/site03-007-start-probe.py, explored the already-inspected two-camera 007 data. It matched each tracked floor observation to a surviving raw projected detection at the same frame by numerical position, then looked backward at most two consecutive frames, requiring adjacent box IoU at least 0.7 and uniqueness among surviving projected detections. It discarded candidate detections already represented by tracked floor observations and candidates claimed by multiple track starts. This is an exploratory rule chosen after inspecting this example, not a frozen held-out evaluation; unprojectable or filtered detections were not included in its ambiguity check.

The probe found 14 predecessor leads: six from C0001055 and eight from C0001056. Among those, 12 and their track-start anchors were each uniquely within 0.5 scene units of the same published reference ID; two were unresolved. This reference-proximity check is neither independently verified camera identity nor false-link accuracy. The two leads before C0001056 track 47 occur at frames 344 and 345, with adjacent box IoU 0.872 and 0.833. This can supply earlier review evidence but does not resolve the later cross-camera handoff or establish a region transition. No observations, IDs, event counts or product behavior were altered.

The ignored output data/processed/site03-007-start-probe.json records candidate coordinates, IoUs and script hash, but the prototype does not yet preserve a complete machine-readable input-hash ledger or independent review and must not be treated as a production-ready importer. Before reuse: harden provenance and ambiguity handling for all detections, add meaningful tests, evaluate other clips without selecting thresholds on their references, and expose tentative evidence without silently joining identities.
