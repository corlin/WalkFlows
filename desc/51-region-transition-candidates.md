# Conservative region transition candidates

The research page now summarizes ordered pairs of configured regions or points. A candidate requires an unambiguous confirmed exit from one item followed by an `enter_candidate` for a different item, strictly later and within 30 seconds. The display reports candidate occurrences and distinct research hypotheses, with buttons that seek to the destination confirmation frame. The report export includes both the pair totals and each event's hypothesis ID, origin exit and destination arrival frames and times.

A gap clears the pending origin; overlapping confirmed regions, multiple same-frame events and simultaneous exit/arrival are treated as ambiguous and do not produce a candidate. Initial or resumed observations inside a destination do not establish entry. The active analysis frame window applies, and the first observation in that window never invents an entry. These rules do not prove the route between regions, global identity or deduplicated people, and may miss real transitions.

Tests cover a synthetic positive path, time limit, gaps, overlap, same-frame ambiguity and initially observed destinations. The page was checked for its empty-layout state. On the current sample, a simple north/south two-rectangle division produced zero candidates under these strict settings; this is not proof of no movement and highlights the impact of missing observations and the selection of example boundaries. More complete observations and independent evaluation are needed before treating the resulting metrics as business statistics.
